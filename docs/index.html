Dialectal English Generation Gap
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

DiaLLM investigates the gap between understanding and generating dialectal English, introducing a model that continually pretrains on the International Corpus of English. The results show that dialectal robustness and generation are dissociated, with benchmarks shaped by pretraining and alignment reshaping generation in ways benchmarks do not capture.

This research matters because it addresses the limitations of large language models in generating dialectal English, which is essential for creating more inclusive and diverse language models.
