Continuous Latent Language Model
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

AURORA-LM is a language model that uses continuous latent spaces for text generation, unlike traditional discrete token-based models. It achieves this by separating the construction of a decodable text representation from the modeling of its distribution, allowing for high-capacity and prefix-aligned latent sequences.

AURORA-LM's approach to continuous latent language modeling could improve the fidelity and coherence of generated text, making it a valuable tool for natural language processing and generation tasks.

GradCuit Improves Latent Reasoning
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

GradCuit is a method that enables robust and interpretable test-time latent reasoning in large language models. It achieves this by inserting optimizable latent states at a selected Transformer layer, allowing for direct credit assignment and improved reasoning trajectory.

GradCuit's ability to provide transparent and efficient latent reasoning can improve the performance and interpretability of large language models in various applications

Unified Embeddings
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

UEmbed is a decoder-only multimodal embedding model that produces sparse and dense representations in one pass. It outperforms existing models on the MMEB-v2 benchmark, achieving 71.8 and 71.0 on dense and sparse tasks respectively.

UEmbed's unified approach to sparse and dense multimodal embeddings can improve the efficiency and effectiveness of retrieval-augmented generation and search systems
