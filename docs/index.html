Benchmark for Medical MLLM Hallucinations
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

ClinHallu is a benchmark for diagnosing stage-wise hallucinations in medical multimodal large language models. It contains 7,031 instances with structured reasoning traces to identify hallucination sources and measure the impact of corrections on final answers.

This benchmark matters because it enables the development of more trustworthy medical language models by identifying and addressing specific sources of hallucinations in the reasoning process.

AdaSR Adaptive Reasoning
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

AdaSR is a framework that enables models to reason during input streaming and perform final deliberation once the stream is complete. It learns when to think and how much computation to allocate across different stages using Hierarchical Relative Policy Optimization.

This matters for building more efficient and flexible language models that can handle dynamic, real-world scenarios like audio and video streams
