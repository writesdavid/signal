TurnSight Improves Tool Reasoning
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

TurnSight is a framework for improving tool-integrated reasoning in large language models through turn-level hindsight self-distillation. It derives supervision from execution-conditioned hindsight and selects reliable signals through cross-horizon agreement.

This matters because it enables more efficient and effective training of language models for complex tasks that require iterative tool interactions.
