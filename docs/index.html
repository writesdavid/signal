Researchers introduced IAAN, a method to improve large audio-language models' performance on non-semantic speech attributes without retraining. IAAN identifies and amplifies key neurons in the audio encoder, resulting in a 25.7% average accuracy improvement across ten attributes.

IAAN's ability to enhance audio models' performance on fine-grained speech attributes without retraining has significant implications for applications like emotion recognition and speaker analysis.
