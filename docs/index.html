Hindcast Evaluates LLM Forecasters
arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

Hindcast is a method for evaluating LLM forecasters by replaying prediction markets at a chosen past date. It grades a model's foresight by limiting its access to information from before the outcome existed, and scoring its forecasts against actual outcomes and market prices.

Hindcast provides a more accurate evaluation of LLM forecasters, helping to identify models that can truly predict future events rather than simply recalling existing information.

arXiv · ●●●○○ Significant
›

Deep Interaction is a method for correcting errors in large language models by allowing users to directly edit the original response. This approach enables precise correction of reasoning errors while preserving accurate steps, and then refines the edited response into a distilled prompt to steer the model along the corrected path.

This method matters because it enables more efficient and effective human-AI collaboration, reducing the time and tokens required to correct errors in complex reasoning tasks.
